# Refund Policy

We want billing to be fair. This policy covers when you're eligible for a refund and how to request one.

## Eligibility

- Monthly plans: you may request a refund within 14 days of the charge for the current billing cycle, provided usage is under 10% of your plan's shipment allowance.
- Annual plans: refunds are available within 30 days of renewal, prorated for unused months, minus a 10% administrative fee.
- Overage charges and label purchases are non-refundable once the shipment has been created.

## How to request

Open a support ticket from Settings > Support with the subject "Refund request" and include the invoice number. We respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

## What isn't covered

We don't refund for carrier-caused delays, incorrect addresses entered by the sender, or service interruptions covered separately under our SLA credits. Downgrades are not refunded retroactively — they take effect next cycle.

## Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, reply to the ticket and it will be escalated to the billing lead for a second review. Chargebacks filed with your bank while a ticket is open will pause the review until the chargeback is resolved.
