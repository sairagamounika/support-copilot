# Resetting Your Password

If you can't sign in, you can reset your password from the login page. You need access to the email address on your account.

## Request a reset link

1. Go to the ParcelPilot login page and click "Forgot password".
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Check your inbox for a message from noreply@parcelpilot.io.

Reset links expire after 60 minutes. If yours expired, request a new one — only the most recent link is valid.

## Choose a new password

Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character. You cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.

## Troubleshooting

If the email doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder and confirm the address is spelled correctly. Corporate mail filters sometimes quarantine automated mail — ask your IT team to allowlist noreply@parcelpilot.io. If you no longer have access to the email on file, contact support with a photo ID and your account's billing ZIP code to verify ownership.

## After resetting

All active sessions except the current one are revoked for security. If you use API keys, they are unaffected by a password reset.
