# Billing and Invoices

ParcelPilot bills on a monthly subscription cycle. Your billing date is the day of the month you first subscribed, and invoices are generated automatically at 00:00 UTC.

## Plans and pricing tiers

We offer three plans: Starter ($49/month, up to 500 shipments), Growth ($149/month, up to 5,000 shipments), and Enterprise (custom pricing, unlimited shipments). Overage is billed at $0.08 per shipment on Starter and $0.05 on Growth.

## Updating your payment method

Go to Settings > Billing > Payment Method to add or replace a credit card. We accept Visa, Mastercard, and Amex. Changes take effect on the next invoice; the current cycle is never re-billed.

## Proration

When you upgrade mid-cycle, we apply proration: you are charged only for the remaining days at the new plan rate, minus credit for unused days on the old plan. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next cycle and are not prorated.

## Net-30 terms

Enterprise customers can request net-30 payment terms. Invoices under net-30 are due 30 days after the issue date. A 1.5% monthly late fee applies to overdue balances.

## Failed payments

If a charge fails, we retry after 3 days and again after 7 days. Your account enters a grace period during retries; after the second failed retry the account is suspended until the balance is paid.
